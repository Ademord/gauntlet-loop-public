# Design basis and limits

Read for provenance. Findings, workflow inferences, implementation checks and task-performance results are distinct. No general advantage is established; historical assessments remain archived.

## Full-text assessment, 30 October 2026, v5.3

Seven full papers were read with appendices; source hashes and exact figure/table coverage are in the scan. Reading is not reproduction.

- **[Humanize](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08900v2)**, 6 October, revised 8 October: observational postmortems expose unsupported completion claims and substantial post-acceptance review. Keep bounded review and prior finding evidence in fresh review. No controlled ablation proves more rounds or different vendors help; most relevant rules already existed in 5.2.
- **[RiseHarness](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.27435v1)**, 20 October: contrasts same-configuration successes/failures using task and runtime evidence. Adapt optional between-run diagnosis with stable-success controls and observable source mappings. Its best-on-transfer and final-development-selected candidates differ; costs and outcomes are not uniformly comparable. Report selection, controls, trial denominators and per-task losses. No imported mechanism or score establishes a gain here.
- **[TRACE](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33517v1)**, 27 September: separates admissible memories from sufficient evidence for a returning role. Adapt action-scoped obligation/dependency coverage, allowing authorized investigation while unsupported actions wait. Implicit invalidation remains model dependent, some gains cost more tokens and printed pooled baseline components have inconsistencies. Missing unrelated obligations must not paralyze useful work.
- **[CALM](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.38573v1)**, 28 October: preserves history separately from query-time admission. Add paired current/historical controls to existing 5.2 behavior, not a memory store. Heuristics can miss implicit conflicts; limited benchmarks and query-time costs do not qualify production use or total cost.
- **[Right Answers, Wrong States / OffQuery](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01244v1)**, 1 October: a correct answer can leave shared information wrong. Add affected retained-state postconditions beside output checks. Controlled episodes, sampled state scoring and judge disagreement limit transfer; a main-text agreement claim conflicts with its detailed table. No whole-workspace audit or production guarantee follows.
- **[SciValidityBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34767v1)**, 26 October: holds evidence content fixed while varying governing conditions. Adapt evidence-use eligibility beyond byte integrity, with decisive-change and irrelevant-change controls. Synthetic finite-choice tasks do not qualify automatic extraction of real conditions or require a graph.
- **[More Memory, Worse Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.27406v1)**, 19 October: retained inferred advice can reproduce errors in tested settings. Keep task-derived advice temporary until admitted; descendants influenced by it are not independent confirmation by themselves. Selective retention helps a baseline; provenance associations are not causal proof and call counts are not dollars/tokens. Do not delete valid facts, user rules or admitted lessons.

Falsifiers include blocking valid historical answers or harmless reads, ignoring a decisive governing change, accepting damaged required state, promoting advice from its own descendants, or calling evaluation-selected results untouched transfer. Preserve failures and matched-context controls. Scenario success qualifies only those decisions.

These domain-specific papers/reports do not qualify concurrent coding memory, universal orchestration or online evolution here. A test failing on an original artifact alone does not validate its expectation. More preparation/curation can cost resources and mislead. Original detailed assessments remain archived.

## Engineering choices

Budgets, reserves, ownership, evidence identities, ladder thresholds, topology, retrieval/admission and resume decisions are adjustable choices, not measured optima. The skill cannot itself enforce billing, semantic truth, test protection or interruption. Capability/model discovery remains live; no vendor ranking, persistent swarm, new database or autonomous business goal follows.
