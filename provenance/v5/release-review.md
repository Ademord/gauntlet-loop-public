# Gauntlet Loop v5 release verification

Date: 2026-09-17. Scope: turn six September 2026 research items into a v5.0.0 package with a thesis, research notes, a research-program proposal, provenance, and tooling, on branch `codex/v5-adaptive-evidence-2026-09-17` of this repository. Benchmarking and installation were not requested and did not happen.

The v4 package was preserved byte-for-byte under `versions/v4/` before any edit (`diff -r` clean; hashes match `provenance/v4/package-verification.json`). The six papers were read at abstract level only, fetched from arxiv.org and recorded verbatim under `research/scan-2026-09-17/`.

The upgrade was run as a gauntlet governed by v4 (`gauntlet/v5-upgrade-2026-09-17/contract.yaml`): nine checks frozen before building, v4 as the bar, six scenario probes, a lead that built, and independent critics dispatched through the harness's subagent facility with fresh context and no access to the lead's transcript. Six critic reviews in three rounds, well inside the 24/30 run allowance; request telemetry is unobservable in this harness and recorded as `unknown`, with harness-reported subagent token counts kept per review as the only cost signal.

- Round 1 (R1 fidelity, R2 probes/consistency/compatibility, R3 prompt-mode on a different model): fidelity passed with every title, identifier, date, and figure matching the re-fetched abstracts; probes passed 6 of 6; compatibility passed; prompt-mode passed with a 449-word compliant prompt; consistency failed on one mechanical deadlock (lesson activation) and five underspecified rules.
- Round 2 (R4 consistency rejudge, R5 prompt-mode redraft): all six round-1 conflicts verified fixed; two new minor conflicts introduced by the exclusive wording of the fixes; prompt-mode passed again and found that the per-piece hard limit could consume the integration reserve under a small cap.
- Round 3 (R6 targeted consistency): both round-2 conflicts verified fixed; one corroborated residual (the "unresolved HOLD" wording in the software-quality SHIP rule and the prompt template) prescribed as a two-word fix. The lead applied it, added an explicit advisory disposition to the HOLD list and an H = 1 clause to the budget rule, and verified by grep and the deterministic checks. No critic re-read the package after those final edits; acceptance of the consistency check rests on R6 plus that verification, and this is disclosed.

Deterministic checks on the final package: `tools/validate_package.py` (8 files, name and version, 11 local links, 3 YAML examples, entrypoint 15,989 of 16,000 bytes, export under 90,000 bytes, forbidden-pattern scan clean), the bundled skill-creator `quick_validate.py`, `tools/build_release.py` (reproducible ZIP, byte-identical on rebuild), and `tools/verify_archive.py` (29 preserved sources, v4 and v5 release files, exports, and local links). Hashes are in `provenance/v5/package-verification.json`; per-round candidate hashes are under `gauntlet/v5-upgrade-2026-09-17/evidence/`.

Not done: installation into the working skill copy (`provenance/v5/installation.json` records `installed: false` and the procedure), merge to `main`, any benchmark, model sweep, or measured superiority claim, and reading the full papers (backlog B-011). Two v4 inheritances flagged by the prompt-mode critic and the template-length residual are recorded in `research/program/backlog.md` rather than forced into this release.
