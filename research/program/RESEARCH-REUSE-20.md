## Reuse decisions

- **Adopt without another effectiveness campaign:** the existence of self-refinement, strong retry controls, cost/quality reporting, preserving negative results, a failure taxonomy, and independent verification practices.
- **Reuse a result directly only when the needed claim matches:** task population, model/effort, tool/feedback access, intervention, resource accounting and acceptance criteria. Otherwise document the mismatch.
- **Shrink to a bridge:** the exact V1/current-host/current-task comparison. Keep its competent controls inside that one bridge; do not create separate campaigns for configurations 1, 2 and 3.
- **Defer:** role/topology sweeps, late-review rules with no eligible runs, cross-run memory without a relevant corpus, and learned routing without enough independent observations.
- **Do not claim settled from this review:** exact Gauntlet performance, current-model optimal orchestration, economic benefits of this repository's checkpoint/ownership policies, or customer willingness to pay. Engineering properties can often be validated offline without a model experiment.

Fresh-context review is a separate viewpoint, not automatically independent evidence. Feedback from actual execution, a human, another model and the held-out answer key are different resources.

## Capital accounting

Earlier proposal: $10 readiness + $54 screening + $36 confirmation = **$100 reserved execution capacity**.
Revised initial bridge, only if transfer gaps justify it: $10 readiness + four tasks × three arms × $3 = **$46**.

The difference is **$18 of proposed screening allocation removed** and **$36 of confirmation deferred**. It is not $54 of observed savings, and the $18 is a reduction in a ceiling, not a measured counterfactual bill. Smaller screening also gives less evidence; it buys a local decision, not statistical confirmation.

Realized net savings would require a documented like-for-like counterfactual execution quote minus actual reuse/bridge/research/evaluation costs. That counterfactual and all attributable research costs are not available here. Report net savings as **not measured**.
